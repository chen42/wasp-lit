# GWAS Catalog Human Genetic Associations

This file summarizes human genome-wide association records captured from the NHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog. Mapped genes in the GWAS Catalog are assigned by genomic proximity and do not establish causal gene identity without functional validation.
